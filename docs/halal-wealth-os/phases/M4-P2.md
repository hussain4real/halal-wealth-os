# M4-P2 — Purification obligations and recorded payments

Status: IN_PROGRESS, unaccepted; local engineering complete and publication gates pending. Sole implementation authority recorded in STATE.json.

M4-P2 - Purification obligations and recorded payments
Milestone: M4 - Shariah evidence and investment decisions.
Dependencies: M4-P1. When crossing milestones, the previous milestone gate must also be accepted.
Initial status: PLANNED. Owner authorization and dependency acceptance determine readiness.
Description: Track sourced purification instructions for relevant distributions separately from portfolio value and cash movements.
Goal: Make an obligation explainable and prevent its recording or payment from double counting cash.
Tasks:
M4-P2-T1 Attach a sourced method, applicable income base, rate or fixed amount and assessment version to an obligation.
M4-P2-T2 Link obligations to recorded distributions and preserve native currency and calculation precision.
M4-P2-T3 Track outstanding, partially paid and settled status through allocations to actual charity-payment journal entries.
M4-P2-T4 Prevent the same distribution/method version from creating duplicate obligations on retries.
M4-P2-T5 Version corrections to the basis or rate and show their effects without overwriting payment history.
M4-P2-T6 Explain missing instructions and keep zakat calculations outside this phase.
Acceptance criteria:
M4-P2-AC1 A synthetic sourced rule of 2% on NGN 100 gross income produces NGN 2 obligation; market value is not substituted as its base.
M4-P2-AC2 Creating an obligation does not debit cash; recording a linked NGN 2 payment debits cash exactly once.
M4-P2-AC3 A missing rate or applicable base leaves the obligation pending rather than declaring no purification due.
M4-P2-AC4 Partial payments reconcile to outstanding balance and cannot be allocated beyond the actual payment amount.
M4-P2-AC5 Duplicate events and rate revisions preserve attribution and do not duplicate obligations or erase payments.
Deliverables: Purification records; calculation service; payment linkage; audit and reconciliation tests.
Verification emphasis: Calculation base, obligation/payment separation, partial allocation, missing instruction and retry tests.
All applicable global invariants and the verification contract also apply. Use the run template to record observed evidence; a checklist tick alone is not proof.
Scope exclusions: Zakat engine, unsourced purification rates, charity transfers and automated investment liquidation.

Apply only recorded source methodology. Synthetic test rates are examples, not religious guidance.
