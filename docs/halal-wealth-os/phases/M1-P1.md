# M1-P1: Accounts, currencies and exact money

Status: owner ACCEPTED; see [acceptance](../runs/M1-P1-acceptance.md). Depends on accepted
M0-P3 and the human-accepted M0 milestone. Canonical phase text restored from
the original October plan; raw retrieval evidence remains private.

Define the account model and exact numeric primitives shared by every later
feature. Goal: store and transport money without floating-point loss or
ambiguous currencies. Keep account balances derived; quantities and prices
require more precision than cash settlement.

## Tasks

- M1-P1-T1: Create bank, cash, investment and liability account types plus internal ledger-account types.
- M1-P1-T2: Store cash amounts in integer minor units; use exact decimal strings for units, unit prices and FX rates.
- M1-P1-T3: Define currency exponents, rounding rules, precision limits and API serialization without JavaScript Number loss.
- M1-P1-T4: Scope accounts to the owner and prevent deleting accounts with posted history.
- M1-P1-T5: Add manual opening-balance inputs routed through the future posting service, not editable balance fields.
- M1-P1-T6: Document asset/liability signs and exclude internal clearing accounts from displayed net worth.

## Acceptance

- M1-P1-AC1: QAR 0.10 plus QAR 0.20 equals QAR 0.30 exactly; values beyond JavaScript safe-integer range round-trip as strings.
- M1-P1-AC2: An NGN amount cannot be added to QAR without an explicit conversion; unsupported currency codes are rejected.
- M1-P1-AC3: Precision/overflow validation rejects invalid inputs before persistence and never truncates silently.
- M1-P1-AC4: Account balances are read-only derived values; archived accounts retain their history.
- M1-P1-AC5: Cross-owner account access is denied and liability display signs match the documented convention.

Deliver money/currency primitives, account schema/UI and ownership/precision
tests. Verify arithmetic, serialization, currency mismatch, overflow, ownership
and account lifecycle. All applicable invariants and validation apply.

Excluded: live FX providers, trading, bank synchronization, mutable balance
shortcuts, M1-P2 posting/idempotency/reversal and M1-P3 FX behavior. Opening
inputs must stay explicitly unposted until the future posting service exists.
Use accepted ADR directions for the transitional representation; document any
remaining gap. Use synthetic fixtures, PHP8.5, full starter/Pest5 TIA and real
uncached full verification against separately inspected disposable PostgreSQL.
Stop READY_FOR_REVIEW or BLOCKED after at most six scoped cycles. No commit,
push, PR, merge, deployment, external publication or self-acceptance.
