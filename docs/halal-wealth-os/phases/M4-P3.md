# M4-P3 — Opportunities, theses and tranche plans

Status: authorized and in progress, unaccepted. M4-P2 is owner accepted.
The private canonical specification controls this public-safe paraphrase.

Goal: retain investment intent, its evidence and review history separately from
cash movements and executed holdings.

- M4-P3-T1: Keep owner-scoped opportunities and watchlists, with versioned
  rationale, risks, horizon and review triggers.
- M4-P3-T2: Plan maximum native-currency allocation and conditional tranches;
  link explicit reservations of existing cash without posting an investment.
- M4-P3-T3: Retain offer-status, price and eligibility sources, verification
  dates and freshness limits. Missing evidence stays unknown.
- M4-P3-T4: Explain allocation limits and configurable currency-concentration
  warnings, including the source and date of their reference totals.
- M4-P3-T5: Attribute later actual subscription or purchase events to the
  retained decision and thesis version without duplicating a financial event.
- M4-P3-T6: Support an explicitly opted-in external private example. Public
  fixtures use independently chosen illustrative values and names.

Acceptance criteria:

- M4-P3-AC1: The original private two-tranche proposal reconciles to its target
  and creates no holding. A separate public synthetic translation tests the
  same exact-money invariant.
- M4-P3-AC2: Missing current official offer evidence is unverified, and missing
  Shariah evidence remains pending review.
- M4-P3-AC3: Tranche reservations cannot reuse cash already protected by an
  emergency fund, another goal or another tranche.
- M4-P3-AC4: Revisions preserve prior reasoning, source evidence and review dates.
- M4-P3-AC5: Plans create neither postings nor shares. Only separately recorded
  actual investment events affect financial records.

Deliverables: opportunity and thesis UI, exact tranche planning, shared cash
reservation linkage, retained decision history, owner isolation, concurrency,
retry and actual-event attribution tests, and rendered desktop/mobile evidence.

No current offer advice, broker submission, price prediction, automatic trade
recommendation, live integration, deployment or M5 work is in scope. Stop for
human M4-P3 acceptance after the authorized engineering and publication gates.
