## Technical conclusions

`InvestmentReplay` rebuilds each account from its selected immutable opening and effective-date/stable-order events. Cost allocation uses exact rational minor-unit arithmetic; a final disposal consumes the remaining minor units. Unknown basis stays unavailable rather than becoming zero. Backdated correction/reversal replays affected history, preserves original facts and validates cash prefixes.

IPO intent, earmark, payment, confirmed allotment, refund and cancellation remain distinct. Payment creates a paid receivable, with no holding units. Only confirmed allotment enters positions; allotment and refund consume the receivable once. Cash effects, investment facts, links and retry receipts commit in one owner-locked PostgreSQL transaction, with deferred database cash-control checks.

Detailed mode selects posted cash, priced positions and paid IPO receivables. Aggregate mode selects one reviewed whole-account value and excludes the underlying detailed components. Missing prices/FX produce partial or unavailable results. Saved reports retain inputs; price selection uses both observed/effective cutoffs and retained raw split context. Cost uses transaction-date FX pools while current value uses reporting-date FX. Allocation explicitly names its covered signed denominator. Unsupported cash flows suppress percentage return.

Global invariants INV-01 through INV-12 were assessed for applicability; future compliance certification remains outside M3. Ownership inspection covered controller/service/nested export paths, including foreign-owner administrator attempts.
